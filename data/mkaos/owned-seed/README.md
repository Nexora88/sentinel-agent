# MKAOS first-party Turkish seed corpus (candidate)

This small seed set was authored specifically for this repository to exercise tokenizer parity, provenance handling, and the from-scratch training pipeline. It is not copied from Common Voice, Wikipedia, Tatoeba, a pretrained model, or another external corpus.

- Intended license: CC0-1.0 for these original seed texts.
- Corpus: `seed-corpus.jsonl`.
- Per-sample UTF-8 SHA-256 and split assignments: `seed-manifest.json`.
- Status: **candidate only; human content/privacy/license review pending**.
- The training gate must remain closed until a maintainer completes review and records approval in the source register and manifest.

This corpus is deliberately tiny and is not sufficient to claim useful Turkish assistant capability. It must not be presented as a production corpus or as legal clearance. The train/validation/test splits are separated by sample ID and content hash; near-duplicate review is still required before approval.
